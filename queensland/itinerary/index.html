Out in Cairns

Gilligan's has an open-air beer hall holding a thousand people. The Woolshed has run themed nights since 1994. Both walkable from the Esplanade. Last drinks 3am, the whole night runs earlier than you are used to, so be out by 10.

Fri 16 OctCairns → Atherton Tablelands~190 km with the loop · 4 h with stops
Drive
Gordonvale → Gillies Range → Atherton

19 km and roughly 260 bends, climbing from the coast onto the tableland. Slow in a campervan.

On offer today

Nerada Tea Plantation, Malanda45 minCurrently plannedPhotos

A family of Lumholtz's tree kangaroos lives in trees planted outside the visitor centre, up to five of them. Mornings are the reliable window. Free, and there is a café. Tree kangaroos are close to unseeable anywhere else.

Waterfall circuit, Millaa Millaa2–3 hCurrently plannedPhotos

A 17 km loop off Teresa Creek Road linking Millaa Millaa, Zillie 7.5 km on, and Ellinjaa 3 km after that. Thirty minutes if you only drive it, two to three hours if you swim at each. Millaa Millaa is the one from every shampoo advert. Free, no booking.

Yungaburra platypus platform1 h at duskCurrently plannedPhotos

Wild platypus in Peterson Creek. Dawn and dusk are the windows. Locals send you across the bridge and onto the track beneath it rather than standing on the platform. The information centre posts daily sightings. Free.

Lake Eacham1–2 hPhotos

Volcanic crater lake in rainforest with a swimming pontoon and easy water access. The circuit walk is 3 km, about 45 minutes. Free.

Granite Gorge, Mareeba2 h + 30 min each wayPhotos

Endangered Mareeba rock wallabies that come to your hand for pellets, up to fifty of them in the afternoon. Found nowhere else in the world. Paid entry, and it is the one option here that is a real detour rather than on the loop.

Sleep: NRMA Atherton Tablelands, 141 Herberton Rd.
Sat 17 OctAtherton → Mission Beach147 km · 2 h 10 driving
Drive
Down the Palmerston Highway

Off the tableland to Innisfail, then south on the Bruce Highway. Josephine Falls, Babinda and Etty Bay are all short detours off this line, so they cost minutes rather than hours.

On offer today

Skydive Mission Beachhalf dayPhotos

Australia's highest tandem jump at 15,000 ft and the only one landing on a beach. About 60 seconds of freefall over the reef. Take a morning slot for the weather odds, a scrubbed jump rolls to the afternoon. A$379 base plus roughly A$170 media and a A$30 levy.

Skydive Mission Beach →
Tully River raftingfull dayPhotos

Grade 3–4, pickup from Mission Beach an hour away. Dam-released so water levels are guaranteed year round. A$184 plus a A$35 river levy on the day. Takes the whole day and rules out everything else on this list.

Raging Thunder →
Etty Bay cassowaries1–2 hCurrently plannedPhotos

Wild cassowaries walk this beach daily, the tamest population in Queensland because of the caravan park behind it. Late afternoon is the window. Pick a spot and wait rather than following them, they are large wild birds and can do real damage. Free.

Josephine Falls1 hCurrently plannedPhotos

Natural granite waterslide, 700 m walk from the car park, 8 km off the Bruce Highway. Only when water is low. Obey the signs, people have died here in high flow. Free.

Babinda Boulders1 hPhotos

Creek pools between huge granite boulders, 7 km west of Babinda. Picnic area, changing rooms and toilets. Strong currents and steep drop-offs where the creek narrows. Free.

Pick two, not four. A morning skydive plus Etty Bay late afternoon works. Adding the falls and the boulders on top does not. Rafting takes the whole day on its own.
Sleep: Mission Beach Camping & Caravan Park, 53 Porter Promenade.

On offer today

Big Crystal Creek, Paluma Range1–2 hPhotos

Straight off the Bruce Highway in the Mount Spec section, no real detour. Clear granite pools stepping down the creek, with the swimming area a short walk from the day-use car park. National park camping here too if the day ever needs to stretch. Free to visit.

Jourama Falls1.5–2 hPhotos

Also directly off the highway, on Waterview Creek. A 3 km return track climbs beside the creek to the falls lookout with swimming holes along the way. Composting toilets, tap water, gas barbecues, good birdwatching. Free to visit.

Geoffrey Bay rock wallabies1 h, late afternoonPhotos

Allied rock wallabies come down onto the rocks at the end of the day. A short walk from Nelly Bay. Free.

Nelly Bay or Horseshoe BayflexiblePhotos

Swim and settle. Stinger season has not started yet in October.

One creek stop, not two. This day already ends on a ferry with a booked bed on the other side. Big Crystal Creek and Jourama are twenty minutes apart on the same stretch of highway, so pick whichever the timing favours when you get there.

On offer this morning

Forts Walk4 km · allow 2 hCurrently plannedPhotos

WWII gun emplacements along the ridge, and wild koalas asleep in the gums directly above the track. Grade 3, open 24 hours, and the final climb to the circuit is steep with many steps. Go early, it is cooler, parking is easier and the wildlife is active. Free.

Route 2, Forts Junction to Radical Bay, is closed 24 June to 1 November 2026. The Forts circuit itself is unaffected. Radical Bay is out; Balding Bay is reached from Horseshoe Bay on a different track and is open.

Barbie car2–3 hCurrently plannedPhotos

Open-top hire car, roof off, round the island. Tropical Topless has closed. Isle Hire does the equivalent from about A$126.50, a plain hatchback is around A$74. Check the seat count, four of you may need two cars.
