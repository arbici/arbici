Whitsundays boat not booked. Everything else is built around it: the van goes back the morning of the 20th, the Airlie room is the night of the 22nd, and the Proserpine flight is the 23rd. Those three dates move if the boat does. October is shoulder season but the 18–35 social boats are the ones that fill first, so this is worth doing before the small operators.
Open: we have no way of getting from Brisbane to Woodfordia. It is roughly 70 km, about an hour. The official EFF Express shuttle page currently returns a 404 and the timetable circulating online is last year's, so there may or may not be a 2026 service. Options are a hire car from Brisbane airport (~A$55/day, about A$55 each over four days split four ways), train to Caboolture plus a local bus and a taxi, or an Uber — roughly A$130–180 each way, which split four ways is about A$40 a head. Nobody has booked anything.
