Collect the campervan

Camperman depot, Cairns. Allow an hour for paperwork and the walkaround. A$500 bond on a credit card. Big food shop here — Cape Tribulation has no supermarket and the village store prices accordingly.

Afternoon
Drive north

Captain Cook Highway up the coast past Port Douglas, then the Daintree River cable ferry, which runs continuously. Road narrows and winds after that. Don't drive it after dark — cassowaries and no lighting.

Sleep: campsite booking 1, night 1 of 3. Cape Trib Camping (beachfront) or PK's Jungle Village.
Mon 12 OctDaintree & Mossman Gorge~120 km round trip
Morning
Daintree River wildlife cruise

Go early — crocodiles bask in the morning and the light is better. Solar Whisper is electric and silent so it gets closer without spooking them.

Solar Whisper A$30 · €18 Crocodile Express
Afternoon
Mossman Gorge

Park at the Gorge Centre and take the shuttle in — private cars aren't allowed up the access road. Cold rainforest swimming holes. Check the signage, the river rises fast after rain.

Evening
Jungle Bar at PK's

Open till midnight, boardwalk straight from the bar to Myall Beach.

Sleep: campsite booking 1, night 2 of 3. Cape Tribulation, same park.
Tue 13 OctCape Tribulationno driving
Morning
Horse ride on Myall Beach, 10:00 or 13:30

Cape Trib Horse Rides, running since 1981 and the only beach ride in the region. 90 minutes through rainforest and creek crossings, then onto the sand and into the shallows. Walking pace, no experience needed.

Cape Trib Horse Rides A$130 · €78
Afternoon
Dubuji and Kulki boardwalks

Short mangrove and rainforest walks off the road, both free. Cape Trib lookout for the rainforest-meets-reef view.

Sleep: campsite booking 1, night 3 of 3. Check out tomorrow morning.
Wed 14 OctCape Tribulation → Cairns140 km · 2 h 30
Morning
Drive south

Stop in Port Douglas for Four Mile Beach and lunch. Restock food.

Afternoon
Esplanade Lagoon

Free saltwater pool on the waterfront. There's no swimmable beach in Cairns itself, which is exactly why the lagoon exists.

Evening
Out in Cairns

Gilligan's has an open-air beer hall holding a thousand people. The Woolshed has run themed nights since 1994. Both walkable from the Esplanade. Last drinks 3am — the whole night runs earlier than you're used to, so be out by 10.

Sleep: campsite booking 2, night 1 of 2. Cairns caravan park.
Thu 15 OctOuter Great Barrier Reeffull day, boat
All day
Passions of Paradise

25-metre sailing catamaran to two outer reef sites, picked on the day for conditions from a pool of 24. Wetsuit, prescription masks and guided snorkelling included, plus buffet lunch. This is the trip's only outer-reef water time.

Check in at the Passions desk, Reef Fleet Terminal, 1 Spence St. Reef-safe sunscreen, and take seasickness tablets the night before rather than on the morning.

Passions of Paradise A$275 · €165
Sleep: campsite booking 2, night 2 of 2. Cairns, same park.
Fri 16 OctAtherton Tablelands~200 km · 4 h with stops
Morning
Gordonvale → Gillies Range → Atherton

19 km and roughly 260 bends, climbing from the coast into the tablelands.

Afternoon
Waterfall circuit

Millaa Millaa, Zillie and Ellinjaa on one short loop road. Millaa Millaa is the one from every shampoo advert. Free, no booking.

Evening
Josephine Falls

Natural granite waterslide on the way back down to the coast. Only when water is low — obey the signs, people have died here in high flow.

Sleep: campsite booking 3, one night. NRMA Atherton Tablelands or Lake Tinaroo.
Sat 17 OctMission Beach~130 km · 2 h
All day
Option A — skydive, up to 15,000 ftOptional

Australia's highest tandem jump and the only one landing on a beach. About 60 seconds of freefall over the reef. Take a morning slot for the best weather odds — if it's scrubbed they roll you to the afternoon.

Skydive Mission Beach A$379+ · €230+
All day
Option B — Tully River raftingOptional

Grade 3–4, full day, pickup from Mission Beach an hour away. Dam-released so water levels are guaranteed year-round. A$35 river levy payable on the day. Can't be combined with the skydive.

Raging Thunder A$184+ · €110+
Sleep: campsite booking 4, one night. Mission Beach caravan park.

Mon 19 OctMagnetic Island → Airlie Beach275 km · 3 h 30 + ferry
Morning
Barbie car and the Forts Walk

Hire an open-top car and drive the island with the roof off. Tropical Topless has closed — Isle Hire does the equivalent from about A$126.50, a plain hatchback is ~A$74. Check the seat count: four of us may need two cars.

The Forts Walk: WWII gun emplacements on the ridge and wild koalas asleep in the gums directly above the track. About 90 minutes return, best early or at dusk. Rock wallabies come down at Geoffrey Bay late afternoon.

Afternoon
Ferry back, drive to Airlie

Collect the van in Townsville and head south. Arrive Airlie in the evening.

Sleep: campsite booking 5, one night. Airlie Beach caravan park, in the van. Last van night — it goes back tomorrow morning.

Hand back the van

Camperman Airlie, 1/384 Shute Harbour Rd, keys at Whitsunday Adventures reception. Clean it out and refuel first. Same road as Coral Sea Marina, so it's a short walk or five-minute taxi to check-in. No rush — the boat isn't until two.

Camperman Airlie →
Afternoon
Sail from Coral Sea Marina, 14:00

Two marinas in Airlie. Coral Sea (formerly Abel Point) handles ~80% of on-water tours and almost all overnight boats; Port of Airlie is the other. Your check-in office confirms which — don't assume. Boats do not wait for late guests.

Small bag aboard only. The rest gets stored at the check-in office — confirm that when booking, since the van's gone by then.

Sleep: not a campsite. At anchor, night 1 of 2. In the boat booking.

Whitehaven and Hill Inlet

Seven kilometres of 98% pure silica sand, so fine it doesn't hold heat and you can walk it barefoot at midday. Hill Inlet lookout at the north end for the swirl of white and turquoise. Snorkelling at island fringing reefs between stops.

Sleep: not a campsite. At anchor, night 2 of 2.

Evening
The big Airlie night

Everyone on that street got off a boat today. Magnums is the engine of it: beer garden on the main street, live bands, pool tables, giant Jenga, cane toad races on Thursdays, A$5 schooners at happy hour. Beaches is where every boat trip ends up afterwards. Paddy's has a full band Friday and Saturday. Last drinks 3am — Airlie is a designated Safe Night Precinct.

Thursday is toad race night at Magnums.

Sleep: not a campsite. Airlie Beach, two twin rooms. The van is long gone.
