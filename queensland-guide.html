Collect the campervan

Land 8:30 AM. Van pickup ~10:00 AM at Camperman depot, Cairns. Allow an hour for paperwork and walkaround. A$500 bond on a credit card. Big food shop here — Cape Tribulation has no supermarket and the village store prices accordingly.

Camperman Cairns →
Afternoon
Drive north to Cape Tribulation

Captain Cook Highway up the coast past Port Douglas, then the Daintree River cable ferry, which runs continuously. Road narrows and winds after. Don't drive after dark — cassowaries and no lighting. Arrive Cape Trib ~12:30 PM. Several hours until sundown (5–6 PM).

Evening
What to do this afternoon?

Depending on activity, you have time for options ranging from 90 minutes to 2.5 hours. Click below to see what's available.

Mossman Gorge (2–2.5 hours)

Rainforest circuit walk (2.4 km) through lush canopy with cold creek swimming holes. Moderate pace, multiple swim breaks. Departs every 20 min on shuttle.

Turtle snorkel (2 hours + boat)

Half-day reef snorkel at Mackay/Undine Reef. Guaranteed turtles, snorkelling gear and instruction included. Later return to camp, sunset arrival.

Cape Trib Fruit Farm (90 minutes)

Guided walk through tropical orchards with local fruit tasting. Low-intensity, good for easing into the region after travel.

Beach and swimming (flexible, 1–2 hours)

Explore Myall Beach, local swimming holes at casual pace. Light activity to settle in after long flights.

Rest and explore (flexible)

Settle at campsite, cook dinner, explore surroundings at your own pace. No structured activity.

Sleep: Cape Trib Camping (beachfront) or PK's Jungle Village.

All day
Passions of Paradise

25-metre sailing catamaran to two outer reef sites, picked on the day for conditions from a pool of 24. Wetsuit, prescription masks and guided snorkelling included, plus buffet lunch. This is the trip's only outer-reef water time.

Check in at the Passions desk, Reef Fleet Terminal, 1 Spence St. Reef-safe sunscreen, and take seasickness tablets the night before rather than on the morning.

Passions of Paradise A$275 · €165

Natural granite waterslide on the way back down to the coast. Only when water is low — obey the signs, people have died here in high flow.

Mon 19 OctMagnetic Island → Airlie Beach275 km · 3 h 30 + ferry
Morning
Barbie car and the Forts Walk

Hire an open-top car and drive the island with the roof off. Tropical Topless has closed — Isle Hire does the equivalent from about A$126.50, a plain hatchback is ~A$74. Check the seat count: four of us may need two cars.

The Forts Walk: WWII gun emplacements on the ridge and wild koalas asleep in the gums directly above the track. About 90 minutes return, best early or at dusk. Rock wallabies come down at Geoffrey Bay late afternoon.

Afternoon
Sail from Coral Sea Marina, 14:00

Two marinas in Airlie. Coral Sea (formerly Abel Point) handles ~80% of on-water tours and almost all overnight boats; Port of Airlie is the other. Your check-in office confirms which — don't assume. Boats do not wait for late guests.

Small bag aboard only. The rest gets stored at the check-in office — confirm that when booking, since the van's gone by then.

Sleep: at anchor, night one.

The big Airlie night

Everyone on that street got off a boat today. Magnums is the engine of it: beer garden on the main street, live bands, pool tables, giant Jenga, cane toad races on Thursdays, A$5 schooners at happy hour. Beaches is where every boat trip ends up afterwards. Paddy's has a full band Friday and Saturday. Last drinks 3am — Airlie is a designated Safe Night Precinct.

Thursday is toad race night at Magnums.
